LoL Ranks Explained: Tiers, Divisions & LP System

League of Legends' ranked system can look confusing from the outside — tiers, divisions, LP, promo series. Here's what each piece actually means and how climbing works in practice.

The Rank Tiers, From Lowest to Highest

League's ranked ladder runs through nine tiers: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, and Challenger. Each tier reflects a broad skill band — the jump between tiers (say, Gold to Platinum) is meaningfully bigger than the jump between divisions within a tier.

Divisions Within a Tier

From Iron through Diamond, each tier is split into four divisions, numbered IV (lowest) to I (highest). You climb from Division IV up to Division I within a tier before promoting to the next tier up. Master, Grandmaster, and Challenger don't use divisions — instead, players are ranked directly by LP on a single ladder.

How LP (League Points) Works

LP is the point system that tracks your progress within a division. Win games, gain LP; lose games, lose LP. Once you hit 100 LP in a division, you enter a promotion series to advance — win the series and you move up a division (or tier, at tier boundaries).

Promo Series

Promotion series are best-of-3 (or best-of-5 at higher stakes) sets of games you play to officially rank up once you've hit 100 LP. Losing a promo doesn't demote you — you stay at 100 LP in your current division and can retry.

Why Rank Matters for Finding Teammates

Rank isn't just a number — it's the clearest signal of whether a duo partner or squad will actually mesh with how you play. A Gold player and a Diamond player have very different expectations around rotations, objective timing, and macro decisions, even if both are mechanically solid.

If you're trying to climb, playing consistently with a duo partner close to your own rank tends to matter more than raw mechanical skill on either side. FAQ

What's the highest rank in League of Legends?
Challenger is the highest tier, reserved for a limited number of the top-ranked players on each server.

Do ranks reset every season?
Yes, League resets ranks (with some soft reset toward your prior rank) at the start of each ranked season, so everyone re-climbs from a lower point.
