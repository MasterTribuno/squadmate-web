How to Find a LoL Duo Partner (Discord, Reddit & In-Game)

Solo queue in League of Legends can feel like a coin flip — you either get four teammates who play as a unit, or a lobby that falls apart by minute ten. A reliable duo partner fixes that: someone to call picks with, cover lanes with, and actually climb with instead of tilting alone. Here's how to actually find one.

How to Find a Duo Partner In-Game

  • Post-game friend requests: If someone on your team communicated well and played their role cleanly, add them right after the game. Most good duo partnerships start as one lucky solo queue game.
  • Client friend suggestions and clubs: Riot's client occasionally surfaces players you've queued with before — check it instead of only relying on memory.
  • In-game chat at champ select: A quick "anyone want to duo after this" in an all-chat-friendly lobby works more often than people expect.

The limitation is scale — you're relying on chance encounters. SquadMate lets you actively search for a LoL duo partner by rank, role, and language, instead of waiting for a good game to happen.

How to Find a LoL Duo Partner on Discord and Reddit

  • LoL LFG Discord servers: Large, active communities with channels split by region and rank, often with a bot for posting your elo and role.
  • Reddit LFG threads: Recurring posts (weekly or pinned) where players list rank, role, and what they're looking for in a duo.
  • Official Riot forums and regional Discords: Smaller but sometimes higher-signal, especially for finding partners in your exact region and server.

Both channels move fast and have no built-in filtering — you're scrolling through a live feed hoping to catch someone at your rank before they find someone else. SquadMate replaces that scroll with a filtered list of LoL players by rank, role, and language.

How to Find a Duo Partner for Ranked / Duo Queue

Ranked duo queue comes with its own rules and stakes, so a few things matter more here:

  • Match by elo, not just "good player": Riot's ranked duo queue restrictions mean your partner's rank has to be within range of yours — check the current limitations before you commit to someone far above or below your division.
  • Cover complementary roles: A duo of two junglers or two mid laners fights for the same picks. Look for someone whose main role pairs with yours (bot/support, jungle/mid, etc.).
  • Agree on comms expectations early: A duo that shares calls and pings consistently climbs faster than two players who happen to queue together.

FAQ

How do I find a LoL duo partner without Discord?
Post-game friend requests work, but they're slow and inconsistent. An app like SquadMate lets you search directly by rank and role without needing a Discord server.

Can I duo queue with someone at a different rank in League?
Riot limits how far apart duo queue partners' ranks can be — check the current season's restrictions, and look for partners close to your own division to stay eligible.

What's the fastest way to find a new LoL duo partner?
Filtering by rank, role, and region directly — rather than scrolling Discord or Reddit LFG posts — is the quickest way to find someone who's actually a fit.
