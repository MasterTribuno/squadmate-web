How to Find Warzone Teammates (Duo & Squad Guide)

Warzone plays best as a duo, trio, or full squad — solo queuing into randoms is where most bad games start. Here's how to actually find teammates for Warzone, whether you're filling one slot or building a full squad from scratch.

Finding Teammates In-Game

  • Add players from good matches: If a random teammate rotated well and called out enemies, send a friend request right after the match instead of relying on memory later.
  • Check your friends list for who's actually in Warzone right now, not just online on the platform.
  • Use crossplay filters if you're open to playing with people on other platforms — it widens your pool significantly.

Finding Teammates on Discord

Warzone LFG Discord servers work similarly to other game LFG communities — channels split by platform, a bot for posting your rank and mic status, and a lot of scrolling to find the right match. It works, but it's slow, and there's no way to know how someone actually plays until you've already queued with them.
