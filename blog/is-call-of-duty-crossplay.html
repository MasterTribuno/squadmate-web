Is Call of Duty Crossplay? Full Guide by Platform (2026)

Short answer: yes, modern Call of Duty titles support crossplay across PlayStation, Xbox, and PC — including Warzone. But whether crossplay is on by default, how to toggle it, and how older or specific titles behave can vary. Here's the breakdown by platform and title.

Is Warzone Crossplay?

Yes. Warzone supports full crossplay across PlayStation, Xbox, and PC, including Warzone Mobile on supported devices. Crossplay is enabled by default, but it can be toggled off in the in-game settings if you specifically want to play within your own platform's lobby.

Is the Latest Call of Duty Crossplay?

Recent mainline Call of Duty entries — including the current season's title — support crossplay by default across all major platforms at launch. This has been standard for the series since Modern Warfare's 2019 relaunch, and every mainline release since has kept it.

Older Call of Duty Titles: Crossplay by Edition

Crossplay support depends on when a title launched and how actively it's maintained:

  • Black Ops entries (recent): Full crossplay across console and PC, same as other modern titles.
  • Older Black Ops entries (3 and earlier) and pre-2019 titles: Generally do not support crossplay — these predate the series-wide crossplay standard and typically run on separate platform lobbies.
  • WWII-era and other legacy titles: No crossplay — check the specific title's current multiplayer population before assuming there's a live crossplay lobby.

Is EA FC / FIFA Crossplay?

EA FC (the current name for the FIFA franchise) supports crossplay in select modes, primarily between same-generation consoles (e.g., current-gen PlayStation and Xbox) and PC in some modes, but not universally across every mode or older console generations. Cross-generation and full PC crossplay support varies by mode and by yearly release, so check the specific mode (Ultimate Team, Clubs, Volta) before assuming it's available.

FAQ

Is Call of Duty Warzone crossplay on PC?
Yes, Warzone includes PC in its full crossplay pool alongside PlayStation and Xbox.

Which Call of Duty games are not crossplay?
Titles released before the series adopted crossplay in 2019, including older Black Ops and WWII-era entries, generally don't support it.

Is Warzone Mobile crossplay with console and PC?
Yes, on supported devices Warzone Mobile can crossplay with console and PC players.
