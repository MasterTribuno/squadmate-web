How to Find Apex Legends Teammates (Ranked, Discord & In-Game)

A bad third teammate can turn a good Apex run into a quick trip back to the lobby. Whether you're playing casual Battle Royale or grinding Ranked, finding people who actually play well with you — and stick around for more than one match — makes the biggest difference in how much fun the game is. Here's every real way to find Apex Legends teammates, and what to expect from each.

How to Find Apex Teammates In-Game

Apex's built-in matchmaking pairs you with random squadmates by default, but there are a few ways to improve on that:

  • Add recent players: After a good match, check your recent players list and send a friend request before you forget. It's the easiest way to build a small pool of decent teammates over time.
  • Use the in-game LFG panel if your platform offers one, to signal you're looking for a squad rather than solo-queuing into randoms.
  • Party up with platform friends — obvious, but worth checking who's actually online and in Apex specifically, not just logged into the platform.

How to Find Apex Teammates on Discord and Reddit

Outside the game, Discord and Reddit are where most players go looking for a squad:

  • Apex LFG Discord servers — large communities with dedicated channels by platform and rank, often with a bot to post your looking-for-group status.
  • r/ApexLegends and r/ApexLFG-style threads — weekly or pinned LFG posts where players list rank, platform, and mic status.

Both work, but they share the same problem: no filtering, no accountability, and a lot of scrolling to find someone who actually matches what you're looking for. That's the gap SquadMate fills — a filtered list of Apex players by rank and platform, without digging through a Discord server or a Reddit thread.

How to Find Apex Ranked Teammates

Ranked is where teammate quality matters most — a mismatched squad can cost you RP fast. A few things to prioritize when looking specifically for ranked teammates:

  • Match by rank, not just by "good" or "bad" — a Platinum player and a Diamond player have very different expectations of rotations and positioning.
  • Look for players who specify their main legend and role — a squad with three duplicate roles struggles more than one with complementary picks.
  • Prioritize a fixed squad over one-off ranked games — playing consistently with the same two teammates across a split climbs faster than solo-queuing every session.
